/// PROJECT SUMMARY ///

BUILT DIFFERENT.

The Creepy Pants are the first major WKTW project. The design is built around a dark, distorted streetwear aesthetic with a strong connection to the underground side of early internet culture.

Black fabric, aggressive graphics and an intentionally rough visual style make these pants feel less like a basic piece of clothing and more like something pulled straight out of a forgotten 2000s web archive.

This project represents the direction WKTW is moving toward: limited pieces, strange ideas, loud graphics and clothing that doesn't try to look polished.
